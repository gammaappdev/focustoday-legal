Privacy Policy

Focus Today · Last updated 23 August 2026

In short: Focus Today does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. The app has no user accounts and no server. Everything you create — sessions, tasks, lists, and settings — is stored only on your device and never leaves it.

This policy explains how Focus Today ("the app", "we") handles information. It applies to the Focus Today mobile application published by Gamma Development.

Information we collect

We collect nothing. Focus Today has no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising, no tracking, and no user accounts. We operate no servers and receive no information about you or your use of the app.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally so it can work offline and remember your progress. This data stays in the app's private storage on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

WhatWhy
Focus sessions and their historyTo show your statistics and streaks
Tasks and lists you createTo let you organize what you work on
App settings and preferencesTheme, timer lengths, sounds, reminders
Current timer stateSo a running session survives closing the app

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. Because we never receive this data, uninstalling removes it completely — there is no copy for us to delete.

Permissions the app requests

PermissionWhat it is used for
NotificationsTo show the running-timer notification and alert you when a session ends
Alarms and remindersTo end a focus session at exactly the right moment, even if the app is in the background
Foreground serviceTo keep the timer running and play ambient sound while the app is not on screen
VibrationFor haptic feedback and session-end alerts
Motion sensorFor the optional "flip to focus" feature. Sensor readings are used momentarily to detect that the phone was turned over. They are never recorded or transmitted.
InternetReserved for subscription purchases (see below). The app makes no network requests for its core features.

Subscriptions

Focus Today offers an optional paid subscription. If you choose to subscribe, the purchase is processed by Google Play, and subscription status is managed on our behalf by RevenueCat, Inc. We never see or store your payment details — those are handled entirely by Google Play.

To recognize your subscription across reinstalls, RevenueCat processes an anonymous identifier and your purchase receipt. It is not linked to your name, email, or any profile. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

Information collected by Google Play

Google Play may collect information about app installs, crashes, and performance independently of us, under its own policies. We may see this in aggregate through the Play Console, but it does not identify you individually.

Children's privacy

Focus Today is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from anyone — including children — because we collect no information at all.

Data security

Your data is protected by your device's own security. Because nothing is transmitted or stored on a server, there is no remote database that could be breached.

Your rights

Privacy laws give you rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to provide or erase. Your data is on your device and entirely under your control.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change — for example when subscriptions become available — we will update this page and revise the date above.

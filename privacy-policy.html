Privacy Policy

Focus Today · Last updated 5 October 2026

In short: Focus Today does not sell or share your personal data, and has no user accounts, no advertising, and no server of ours. Everything you create — focus sessions, focus areas, and settings — is stored only on your device. The only data that leaves your device is (1) what is needed to process an optional Pro subscription, handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store and RevenueCat (see "Subscriptions and payments"), and (2) anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, handled by Google Firebase (see "Analytics and crash reporting").

This policy explains how Focus Today ("the app", "we") handles information. It applies to the Focus Today mobile application published by Gamma Development.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, or any account information. Focus Today has no advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no user accounts. We operate no servers of our own and do not receive the focus sessions, focus areas, or settings you create.

Two kinds of data are processed by third parties on our behalf and described below: subscription processing, and anonymous analytics and crash reporting. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally so it can work offline and remember your progress. This data stays in the app's private storage on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

WhatWhy
Focus sessions and their historyTo show your statistics and streaks
Focus areas you create (name and color)To let you label what you are focusing on and see time per area in your statistics
Your free-trial start date and Pro statusTo know whether the trial is active and whether Pro features are unlocked
Your choices for Block Apps and strict mode (such as your allowed-apps list)To apply the focus restrictions you set
App settings and preferencesTheme, language, timer lengths, sounds, reminders
Current timer stateSo a running session survives closing the app

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. Because we never receive this data, uninstalling removes it completely — there is no copy for us to delete.

PermissionWhat it is used for

Alarms and reminders (exact alarms)To end a focus session at exactly the right moment, even if the app is in the background
Foreground serviceTo keep the timer running and play ambient sound while the app is not on screen

Subscriptions and payments

Focus Today offers a free trial and an optional paid subscription ("Pro"), billed monthly or annually. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). We never see, receive, or store your card number or other payment details. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your Google Play or App Store subscription settings, or from the Manage button in the app.

Subscription status is managed on our behalf by RevenueCat, Inc. To do this, the RevenueCat SDK in the app sends the following to RevenueCat's servers:

DataWhy
A randomly generated anonymous app user IDTo identify your subscription without an account
Purchase receipt / transaction details and subscription statusTo verify your purchase, unlock Pro, and let you restore it after reinstalling or on a new device
Standard technical data (such as app version, device platform and OS version, locale, and IP address)To operate the service and prevent fraud

This data is not linked to your name, email address, or any profile we hold, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking. Your focus sessions, focus areas, and settings are never sent to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's privacy policy, Google's privacy policy, and Apple's privacy policy.

Analytics and crash reporting

To understand which parts of the app are used and to find and fix crashes, Focus Today uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google LLC. They process data on our behalf. The data is anonymous: it is not linked to your name, email address, or any account, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

DataWhy
Usage events, such as which screens you open, when the app is opened, and how long a session lastsTo understand how the app is used and improve it
Crash reports: the technical error details and the state of the app and device when it crashedTo find and fix crashes and errors
A randomly generated app-installation ID created by FirebaseTo tell installs apart and count users without identifying you. It is reset when you uninstall the app.
Standard technical data (such as device model, operating system version, app version, language, and approximate region derived from your IP address)To group results by device and version and to diagnose problems

We do not send your focus sessions, focus areas, task names, settings, or any text you type to Firebase. The app does not collect your device's advertising ID or other persistent device identifiers for analytics. Analytics event data is kept for up to 14 months. Uninstalling the app stops all collection. For how Google handles this data, see Firebase privacy and security and Google's privacy policy.

Information collected by Google Play and Apple

Google Play and the Apple App Store may collect information about app installs, purchases, crashes, and performance independently of us, under their own policies. We may see aggregated reports through the Play Console and App Store Connect, but they do not identify you individually.

Children's privacy

Focus Today is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app has no accounts or profiles, and the only data processed off your device is the anonymous subscription, analytics, and crash data described above.

Data security

Your focus data is protected by your device's own security. We keep no database of it, so there is nothing on our side that could be breached. Subscription, analytics, and crash data is transmitted over encrypted connections and handled by Google, Apple, and RevenueCat under their own security practices.

Your rights

Privacy laws give you rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal data a company holds about you. We hold no personal data about you ourselves: your focus data is on your device and under your control. For subscription, analytics, and crash data held by RevenueCat, Google, or Apple, you can exercise your rights with them using the privacy links above, or contact us and we will help where we can. Because this data is anonymous, we may not be able to identify which records belong to you.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and revise the date above.

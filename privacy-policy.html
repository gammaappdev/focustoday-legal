Privacy Policy

Focus Today · Last updated 4 October 2026

In short: Focus Today has no user accounts and no server of our own, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information. Everything you create — sessions, tasks, lists, and settings — is stored only on your device. The one exception is subscriptions: if you buy Focus Pro, your purchase is processed by Google Play and verified by our subscription provider, RevenueCat, using an anonymous identifier (see Subscriptions).

This policy explains how Focus Today ("the app", "we") handles information. It applies to the Focus Today mobile application published by Gamma Development.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. Focus Today has no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no advertising, no tracking, and no user accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location, and we operate no servers of our own.

The only information that leaves your device relates to optional subscriptions and is described under Subscriptions below.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally so it can work offline and remember your progress. This data stays in the app's private storage on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

WhatWhy
Focus sessions and their historyTo show your statistics and streaks
Tasks and lists you createTo let you organize what you work on
App settings and preferencesTheme, timer lengths, sounds, reminders
Current timer stateSo a running session survives closing the app

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. Because we never receive this data, uninstalling removes it completely — there is no copy for us to delete. (Subscription records are different; see Deleting subscription records.)

Subscriptions

Focus Today offers an optional paid subscription, Focus Pro. We use two services to provide it:

Google Play. Purchases are made and managed through Google Play. We never see or store your payment details — those are handled entirely by Google Play, under Google's privacy policy.

RevenueCat. To confirm whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc., a subscription-management service that acts on our behalf. RevenueCat receives:

  • an anonymous identifier generated for your installation. It is not linked to your name, email address, or Google account;
  • your purchase information from Google Play, such as the product you bought, the purchase and expiry dates, and whether the subscription is active; and
  • technical information that accompanies any network request, such as your IP address, app version, and device and operating system details.

RevenueCat uses this information only to provide subscription services to us and handles it under RevenueCat's privacy policy. We do not use it to identify you, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not sell it.

The app contacts these services only when you open the paywall, buy or restore a purchase, or when it checks whether your subscription is still active. If you never subscribe, nothing beyond that status check is sent.

You can cancel or change your subscription at any time in Google Play (Menu → Subscriptions), or from the Manage button in the app's Settings.

Information collected by Google Play

Google Play may collect information about app installs, crashes, and performance independently of us, under its own policies. We may see this in aggregate through the Play Console, but it does not identify you individually.

Children's privacy

Focus Today is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — including children. The only data involved is the anonymous subscription information described above.

Data security

Your sessions, tasks, lists, and settings are protected by your device's own security and are never stored on a server. Subscription information is sent to RevenueCat over encrypted connections.

Deleting subscription records

Uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription, and purchase records may remain with Google Play and RevenueCat under their own retention policies. To cancel, use Google Play (Menu → Subscriptions).

To ask us to delete the anonymous subscription records we hold with RevenueCat, email support.focustoday@gmail.com and include the Google Play order number from your purchase receipt, so we can find them.

Your rights

Privacy laws give you rights to access, correct, export, or delete personal data a company holds about you. We hold no personal information such as your name or email address. Your sessions, tasks, and settings are on your device and entirely under your control. For the anonymous subscription records described above, you can contact us as explained under Deleting subscription records.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and revise the date above.
